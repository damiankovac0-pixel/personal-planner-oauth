# Personal Planner privacy policy

Effective: 24 October 2026.

Personal Planner is a private integration used by its account owner. It uses the open-source Google Workspace MCP connector locally to access Google Tasks.

## Data access and use
The connector accesses the authorized Google account’s task lists, tasks, completion information, and basic identity to carry out the owner’s requests. Google Calendar and iCloud access through the separate native connector uses macOS account permissions.

## Storage and sharing
OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are retained on the owner’s Mac, not on this website or in this public repository. Data requested through the assistant can enter the assistant conversation and be processed by its configured AI provider under that provider’s terms. This is not an offline AI system. The setup does not sell task or calendar data or use it for advertising.

## Control and deletion
The owner can revoke the Google connection in Google Account third-party connections, revoke native access in macOS Privacy & Security, and remove local credentials and assistant conversation records. This site stores no Google user data.

## Contact
For setup issues, contact the account owner through [the project repository](https://github.com/damiankovac0-pixel/personal-planner-oauth/issues). Never post credentials, tokens, or private calendar/task contents in public issues.
